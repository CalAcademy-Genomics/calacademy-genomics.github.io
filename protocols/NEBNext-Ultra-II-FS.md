# NEBNext Ultra II FS DNA Library Prep Kit

{: .caution}
NEB sells several different kits with very similar names (e.g., the Ultra II FS and Ultra Express FS kits have different protocols). Ensure you are using the proper protocol for your kit!

The link to the manufacturer protocols can be found below:
* [UDI Adaptors (most CCG projects)](https://www.neb.com/en-us/-/media/nebus/files/manuals/manuale6177-e7805.pdf).
* [UDI-UMI Adaptors](https://www.neb.com/en-us/-/media/nebus/files/manuals/manuale6177_e7805-w-umi-adaptors-dna.pdf)

{: .note}
If you are confident in your pipetting skills, NEB protocols can often be scaled down to half, quarter, or even smaller fractions to save resources. Some steps are suitable for "master mixes" to minimize the impact of pipette variability; read the protocol carefully to identify exceptions. The CCG commonly uses half reactions, while reactions below this volume may require tuning.
